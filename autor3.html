About the Author

Matt Haig was born on 3 July 1975 in Sheffield.His mother, Mary, worked as a primary school headteacher and his father, Richard, was an architect, who commuted to London for work. His paternal grandmother was Jewish and escaped from Austria after the Anschluss. Haig grew up with his younger (by three years) sister, Phoebe Rose, in the Nottinghamshire town of Newark. He attended Magdalene High School and Thomas Magnus Upper School in Newark. He has admitted to minor shoplifting during his teenage years and has suffered subsequent guilt from the petty crime, which included the theft of a tennis ball.[4] He went on to study English and History at the University of Hull.
